Third-party Python packages are installed from the exact wheels and SHA-256
hashes in runtime-requirements.lock. Their original *.dist-info directories,
licenses, notices and binary-library license directories remain in
runtime/Lib/site-packages/. BUILD-MANIFEST.json lists every packaged version.

FriBiDi 1.0.18 (LGPL-2.1-or-later) is loaded as a separate, replaceable DLL.
Its license is retained in runtime/fribidi-license/.
The corresponding upstream source archive and actual conda build recipe are
included in runtime/fribidi-source/. The DLL may be replaced independently.
Source: https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi/releases/tag/v1.0.18
Binary package and build recipe: https://anaconda.org/conda-forge/fribidi

Amiri font: SIL Open Font License, app/quran_reels/fonts/OFL.txt.

FFmpeg is NOT contained in this application download. At first launch, the
application downloads the pinned GyanD essentials archive directly to the
user's own computer, verifies its SHA-256, and keeps its license alongside
ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in the user's local application data folder.
It invokes these separate executable programs; it does not link FFmpeg
libraries into the application. No FFmpeg download is uploaded to our releases.
Upstream: https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg/releases/tag/9.0.2
Licensing: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

Runtime downloads and application packages may not be silently replaced.
To update a pinned component, review its version, checksum and license,
rebuild the package, run the documented checks, then publish a new release.
